EARTH

This is where your story begins. After a long sleep you wake up on a overgrown planet. You are surounded by a group of people called the Grineer. The Grineer on this planet is lead by Vor.

The Grineer aren't the only ones on this planet. A group of people not allied with the grineer live in a market village called cetus. Cetus borders the plains of Eidalon where the grineer have set up a few bases. Every night giants roam the plains these giants are the Eidalons, Sentits that were once destroyed rebuild themselves every night roam the plains to go back to there slumber during the day.

Earth has many mysteries to be found as you explore.
